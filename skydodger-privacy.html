Privacy Policy — Sky Dodger

Effective date: June 20, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DodgeLab ("we", "us") handles information when you use the mobile game Sky Dodger (the "App"). We have designed the App to collect as little personal information as possible. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

(a) Information you provide

  • Nickname. If you submit a score to the global ranking, the nickname you choose (default "DIVER") is stored together with your score and level. You are not required to use your real name, and we recommend you do not.

(b) Information collected automatically

  • Advertising identifier (Advertising ID / AD_ID) and related identifiers, used by our advertising provider to deliver and measure ads.
  • Device and technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, IP address, and general (non-precise) location derived from IP, collected by our third-party service providers.
  • Gameplay and usage data such as scores, levels reached, in-app events, and crash/diagnostic information.

(c) Information stored only on your device

  • Your game progress, coin balance, owned items, equipped goggles, and settings are stored locally on your device (local storage). This data is removed when you uninstall the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run core game features (saving progress, displaying global rankings).
  • To display advertisements (including rewarded ads for continuing play) and measure their performance.
  • To process in-app purchases (e.g., remove-ads, coin packs) through Google Play.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance.

4. Advertising & Your Choices

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Locally stored data is kept until you clear the App data or uninstall the App.
  • Ranking entries (nickname, score, level) are retained to operate the leaderboard. To request deletion of a ranking entry associated with you, contact us at the email below.
  • Data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies.
